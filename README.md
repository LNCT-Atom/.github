## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🎯 Niyukti — AI-Powered Placement Management Platform

**Bridging the gap between student preparation and placement opportunities.**

Niyukti is our vision for making campus placements smarter, more organized, and more accessible. It aims to bring students, recruiters, and Training & Placement (T&P) departments together through a unified placement management experience.

Instead of managing resumes, assessments, preparation, and placement updates across disconnected platforms, Niyukti envisions a more connected journey from learning and preparation to shortlisting and selection.

### ✨ What We're Building

- 📄 **Smart Resume Analysis** — Help students understand their resumes and identify opportunities for improvement.
- 🎯 **Intelligent Job Matching** — Connect student profiles and skills with relevant company requirements.
- ✅ **Smarter Shortlisting** — Support placement teams in identifying eligible and relevant candidates.
- 📚 **Personalized Preparation** — Help students discover skill gaps and focus on areas that need improvement.
- 🎤 **AI-Powered Mock Interviews** — Enable students to practice technical and HR interviews and receive structured feedback.
- 📊 **Placement Insights** — Help T&P departments understand student progress, assessment performance, and placement trends.
- 🤝 **Connected Placement Journey** — Bring student profiles, company drives, applications, assessments, and interview progress into one organized experience.

Our goal is to make placement preparation more guided for students and placement management more efficient for institutions.
